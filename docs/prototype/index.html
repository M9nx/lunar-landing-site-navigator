What you are looking at

Each polar plot is a synthetic local sky: outer dark ring is below the geometric horizon, the dashed circle is 0°, the filled shape is terrain. Sun and Earth are centre-ray markers — not disc fractions, power, or radio. Jupyter and rasters stay in the Hub; see JUPYTER_PRACTICE_PASS.md.
