Approach

How the comparison is intended to work

01

Select sites and time

Users compare documented south-polar regions and a sample date. Exact landing coordinates still have to be chosen from archive ROIs.

02

Compute local sky geometry

A future validated pipeline will use SPICE kernels and lunar frames to place the Sun and Earth against the local horizon.

03

Keep terrain and links distinct

Geometric elevation is not terrain-clear sunlight, and Earth visibility is not a radio-link budget. Labels in the demo keep that boundary visible.

Limitation

A reachable API is not a scientific result

Keep synthetic demo labels visible until a reproducible pipeline, validated data products, and benchmark evidence replace them. Data sources and assumptions are documented in the research files. Large mission datasets should be downloaded from their authoritative archives rather than stored in this repository.
